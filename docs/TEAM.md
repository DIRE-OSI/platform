# Team & partners

DIRE is developed through a collaboration between academic, technical and public-health institutions. Roles described below are indicative and may evolve as the collaboration develops.

## Collaborating institutions

| Institution | Indicative role |
|---|---|
| **University of California San Diego**, School of Global Policy and Strategy | Academic leadership; grant recipient |
| **New Light Technologies, Inc.** | Platform engineering, data science, data engineering and operation of the live dashboard |
| **Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia** (UPCH), Peru | Scientific collaboration, surveillance-data expertise and regional public-health context |

## Open-science maintenance

The open repository, this documentation site and the live dashboard's public release notes are maintained by New Light Technologies on behalf of the DIRE project team. Current maintainers are listed in the repository's [CODEOWNERS](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/blob/main/CODEOWNERS) file.

New issues and discussions are acknowledged automatically and reviewed by the maintainers. Items that need code or data work are tracked by the project team and reported back in the original issue, and in the release notes once they are live. DIRE is in beta, so response times may vary.

## Collaborating and getting in touch

Collaboration from researchers, public-health agencies and data partners is welcome.

- **Feedback, problems or ideas about the live dashboard** — use the [dashboard feedback form](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/issues/new?template=dashboard_feedback.yml) (English, Spanish or Portuguese).
- **Technical questions, ideas, reproducibility** — open a [discussion](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/discussions) or an [issue](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/issues).
- **Data partnerships, validation studies or extension to new settings** — these involve institutional agreements; please open a discussion describing your organisation and objectives, and a maintainer will follow up.
- **Release notes** — see [Releases](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/releases); choose **Watch → Custom → Releases** on the repository page to be notified.
- **Security concerns or sensitive matters** — see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Issues and discussions are public and need a free GitHub account. Please don't post personal or patient information.
