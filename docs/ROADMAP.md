# Roadmap

This page outlines an **indicative** development pathway for the open components of DIRE. Sequencing and scope are preliminary and remain subject to review by the collaborating institutions, data availability and partner agreements. No dates are implied.

| Stage | Focus | Indicative status |
|---|---|---|
| **I. Documentation and governance** | Methods, research basis, data sources and data dictionary; licensing, contribution and security policies | In progress |
| **II. Reproducibility resources** | Schema-faithful synthetic test data; worked examples and notebooks | In preparation |
| **III. Open workflows** | Release of covariate-processing and forecasting workflows runnable without restricted data; automated testing | Planned |
| **IV. Archival release** | Tagged, citable releases with persistent identifiers (DOI) | Planned |
| **V. Evaluation reporting** | Publication of evaluation summaries by horizon, administrative level and disease | Planned |
| **VI. Extension** | Additional settings and climate-sensitive diseases, subject to data partnerships | Under consideration |

## Iterative improvement

Development follows an iterative cycle: data and methods are refined in collaboration with in-country partners, evaluated against held-out data and simple reference forecasts, and documented here as they mature. Feedback from researchers and public-health users informs priorities.

## Community input

Suggestions on priorities are welcome through [Discussions](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/discussions). See [Get involved](https://github.com/DIRE-OSI/platform/blob/main/README.md#-get-involved) for ways to participate.
